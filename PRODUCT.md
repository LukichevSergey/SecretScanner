# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and security-conscious teams auditing iOS, macOS, Swift, and other codebases before sharing them with AI services or third parties. They need to select a project, tune audit scope, run it, and immediately understand the outcome.

## Product Purpose

SecretScanner finds accidentally committed keys, credentials, certificates, and other sensitive data. The desktop interface makes a pre-share security audit quick and clear without requiring command-line knowledge.

## Brand Personality

Focused, technical, vigilant. The interface should feel like a capable security console: dark, precise, and energetic without becoming noisy.

## Anti-references

Avoid an oversized empty canvas, dense wall-of-settings forms, decorative neon everywhere, and visual effects that obscure the primary scan workflow.

## Design Principles

- Put the scan action and its current state at the center of the interface.
- Make the configuration path understandable at a glance.
- Use cyberpunk color as a signal for action and risk, not as decoration.
- Keep technical controls approachable with concise labels and useful defaults.
- Preserve native, keyboard-friendly desktop interactions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use high-contrast text and clear state indicators that do not depend on color alone. Respect the platform's normal keyboard navigation and avoid nonessential animation.
